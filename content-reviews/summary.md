## The 5 themes (fix once, apply everywhere)

**1. AHPRA claim balance (must-fix).** Unbalanced claims on every page: "safe, powerful, and lasting", "life-changing", "generally safe", outcome certainty ("your appetite will also be reduced", "consistently achieve better outcomes"). Each per-page review has paste-ready rewrites. **Brief-inherited subset:** the hub's "make these procedures safe for most suitable candidates" and the bypass keyhole "less pain" line implement the briefs near-verbatim — those brief sections need the same correction.

**2. TGA prescription-medicine references (must-fix + a policy decision).** Not just one semaglutide mention: the hub names semaglutide and, with the eligibility page, carries several GLP-1 *class* references. TGA guidance captures direct **and indirect** references to prescription-medicine classes (GLP-1s, "weight-loss injections") in public health-service advertising. The briefs themselves mandate "GLP-1 medical weight management" coverage, so this is a site-wide question — how does the practice want to describe medical weight management? — to settle once (with legal input) and apply to briefs and drafts together.

**3. Medicare/BMI accuracy + cost certainty (must-fix).** Drafts state "BMI 35+, or 30–35 with conditions" next to MBS item numbers; the MBS wording (explanatory note TN.8.29) is BMI 40+, or 35+ with major comorbidities — lower-BMI pathways must read "may be considered for assessment", never "you may qualify". **Brief-inherited:** the sleeve and bypass briefs mandate the 35/30–35 framing — correct the briefs too. Also: item 31581 (BPD/duodenal switch) cited on the Roux-en-Y page (should be 31572 only); single dollar fees quoted against the briefs' no-single-figure rule, plus a literal "$[X,XXX]" placeholder; and absolute insurance claims ("gold-tier… covers/will cover the hospital and theatre component") need policy-, waiting-period- and gap-aware wording.

**4. Leftover working notes in the copy (must-fix, trivial).** "Conversion CTA headline" labels, inline "Alt" headline options, an "Option A" heading, planning notes — and the bypass CTA that says *gastric sleeve*. Must be resolved before build. (The brief + keyword table at the top of each doc is fine — it's clearly reference material, not page copy, and isn't counted against anyone.)

**5. Length + FAQ coverage (should-fix).** Body-only counts incl. FAQs (FAQs count toward the target, as agreed 1 Jul): 1,844 / 1,783 / 2,091 / 1,958 / 1,908 vs targets of 1,000–1,500 — roughly 280–900 over. Decide once: enforce the brief targets (per-page cut lists are in the reviews) or consciously accept ~1,800. While cutting, add the missing FAQ topics Google is currently asking (cost *in Perth*, the 30/30 eating rule, who is *not* eligible, Medicare coverage, 10-year outcomes).
