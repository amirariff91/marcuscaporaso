# Super Access for Weight Loss Surgery — round-2 review (reworked draft)

**Verdict: Rework** · 5 blocking · 4 should-fix · 1 minor

This review covers the writer's reworked draft, checked against every directive from the batch-2 review plus a fresh compliance and quality pass. Eligibility framing of BMI 35+, or BMI 30+ with a related condition, is the practice's confirmed policy and is treated as correct throughout.

**Findings**

1. **Blocking** — `It can remove the cost barrier and let you have surgery sooner, rather than waiting years to save the gap payment.` — compassionate release marketed as a benefits pathway (also `Acting earlier may help address health conditions` and `It can be combined with private health cover or an external payment plan to cover the full cost`).
   **Writer edit:** Replace the "Why some patients choose to apply" list with:
   > Compassionate release exists so that necessary medical treatment is not out of reach for people who cannot otherwise fund it. Whether it is appropriate for you depends on your medical circumstances, your finances and the ATO's strict criteria. The application is lodged directly with the ATO through myGov, and the decision rests with the ATO and your fund, not with the practice.
   **Brief-owner note (OSWA, not the writer):** The brief's Benefits section and "surgery now vs waiting years" line mandate the deleted copy; amend the brief.

2. **Blocking** — `fall within the medical treatment category where a specialist certifies` — the criteria passage still implies specialist certification establishes the pathway; the page never states plainly that surgery is not pre-approved.
   **Writer edit:** After the strict-criteria sentence, add: "Certification by your treating doctors supports an application; it does not pre-approve surgery or guarantee release. The ATO assesses the qualifying criteria and evidence for every application."

3. **Blocking** — `Our admin team can walk you through the numbers at your consultation.` — financial-advice boundary incomplete (also `Our team can talk through what makes sense for your specific situation.`); the page routes only to an external adviser and never to the practice's financial coordinator.
   **Writer edit:** Replace both sentences with: "The financial coordinator can explain the written estimate, the documentation the ATO requires and the practical steps; for the retirement impact of releasing super, speak with a licensed financial adviser. The practice cannot advise whether you should access retirement savings."

4. **Blocking** — `~1700 words incl. FAQ` — scaffolding remains: the metadata block (`Primary keyword:` line, keyword table, semantic terms), the word-count annotation, the empty `###` heading, the bare `CTA` label and the highlight `<span class="mark">` wrappers.
   **Writer edit:** Remove all of the above; publish the H1 "Accessing your superannuation for weight loss surgery in Perth" and the finished CTA "Map out your costs and options".

5. **Blocking** — `Two partner hospitals` — unverified hospital claims (also the scheduling sentence naming Mount Private Hospital and Waikiki Private Hospital).
   **Writer edit:** Hero strip: "FRACS-qualified bariatric surgeons — in-house aftercare team." Step 6: "With funding in place, we schedule your pre-operative assessment and then surgery at the private hospital selected for your care."
   **Brief-owner note (OSWA, not the writer):** Confirm the hospital list; the brief's semantic terms still reference SJOG Murdoch while the draft names different hospitals.

6. **Should-fix** — `14-28 days` — the stats tile merges digital and paper decision times, and the process step (`typically decided within 2-3 weeks`) is out of step with the corrected FAQ 4.
   **Writer edit:** Tile note: "Digital applications usually ~14 days; paper up to 28. Fund processing can add time." Align the step-4 sentence with FAQ 4's wording.

7. **Should-fix** — `every option before you commit to anything` — the funding callout still sells options and could imply a practice payment product.

9. **Should-fix** — `tax is typically withheld before the money reaches you.` — the tax FAQ still lacks the routing sentence.
   **Writer edit:** Append: "Confirm the tax treatment that applies to you with your super fund or a registered tax adviser."

10. **Minor** — `### **How to access super for surgery**` — correct wording, wrong level; promote to an H2 to carry the "access super for surgery" phrasing.
