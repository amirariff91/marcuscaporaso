**Findings**

1. **Blocking** — `eligibility is assessment-based and generally requires a BMI of 35 or higher` — presents the practice's clinical thresholds as the item 31575 Medicare criteria.
   **Writer edit:** Replace the bullet beginning "For Medicare item 31575…" with:
   > Your surgeon will assess whether gastric sleeve surgery is clinically suitable for you. The practice works to a BMI of 35+, or BMI 30+ with an obesity-related condition, but these clinical thresholds do not by themselves establish eligibility for MBS item 31575 or a Medicare rebate. Medicare requirements and any rebate are assessment-based and depend on the relevant service and your circumstances.
