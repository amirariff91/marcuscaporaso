**Findings**

1. **Blocking** — `Applies to MBS items 31569-31581 (bariatric surgery)` — the stats bar states a "Yes" rebate outcome across the whole item range as fact, sweeping in item 31581, and hard-codes waiting-period and agreed-rate outcomes.
   **Writer edit:** Replace the stats bar with:
   > | Label | Value | Note |
   > |---|---|---|
   > | Medicare rebate | Assessment-based | Applicable services, MBS items and rebates vary |
   > | Hospital and theatre | Separate from Medicare | Private-health benefits are policy-dependent |
   > | Waiting period | Policy-dependent | Ask your fund whether a waiting period applies |
   > | Agreed hospital rates | Policy-dependent | Ask whether an agreed rate applies to your policy |
   **Brief-owner note (OSWA, not the writer):** Confirm whether the page should reference only items 31575/31572 or the wider 31569–31581 family; item 31581 must never be implied for bypass.

2. **Blocking** — `plus the anaesthetist, physician, and dietitian consultations connected to your surgery.` — surgical item numbers presented as covering other professional services.
   **Writer edit:** Replace with:
   > Medicare may rebate eligible professional services under applicable Medicare Benefits Schedule (MBS) items. Surgical items include 31575 for sleeve gastrectomy and 31572 for Roux-en-Y gastric bypass; anaesthetist, assistant and other consultation services may use separate items. Which items and rebates apply depends on the individual patient and procedure.

3. **Blocking** — `Medicare *does not* cover your hospital stay, theatre fees, or accommodation.` — definite Medicare/hospital coverage wording repeated through the page instead of the required separation.
   **Writer edit:** Replace each occurrence with:
   > Medicare may rebate eligible professional services, including an eligible specialist consultation. A Medicare rebate does not itself pay private-hospital accommodation or theatre charges; private-health benefits are separate and policy-dependent. Private hospital insurance may contribute to hospital costs when the policy includes bariatric surgery and the relevant items, subject to waiting periods, exclusions, pre-approval, excesses, copayments and fund rules — the tier name alone is not enough.

4. **Blocking** — `Our partner hospitals — Mount Private Hospital and Waikiki Private Hospital — have agreed rates with most major funds, including Medibank, Bupa, HCF, NIB and CBHS.` — unverified hospitals, an unverified `four partner hospitals` count (two are named; page 01 says "three"), named funds and per-fund commercial claims.
   **Writer edit:** Replace all hospital, count, fund and agreed-rate copy with:
   > Once your cover, any required pre-approval and pre-operative requirements are confirmed, the team will confirm the hospital selected for your care and the fund arrangements that apply to your policy before surgery is scheduled. Ask your fund whether an agreed hospital rate applies to your specific policy, provider and selected hospital.
   Remove FAQ 6 entirely.
   **Brief-owner note (OSWA, not the writer):** Verify the hospital list and count, agreed-rate arrangements and participating funds before any are named; the brief still references St John of God Murdoch and Subiaco.
