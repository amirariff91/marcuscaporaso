## Batch 4 — Trust & E-E-A-T pages (first round)

Three pages reviewed: **Our Bariatric Surgeons**, **Hospitals & Clinic Locations**, **GP Referral Information**. All three come back **Rework**.

These are the pages that establish who OSWA's surgeons are, where they operate, and how a GP hands a patient over — so accuracy on names, credentials and addresses matters more here than anywhere else on the site. That is where the problems are.

### What the writer got right

The clinical judgement is sound throughout. The "what to look for when comparing surgeons" section is the strongest single piece of writing in the batch, and it happens to be the most commercially useful too — people searching this term are comparing, not booking. The consulting-rooms-versus-hospital distinction on the locations page is clear and genuinely helpful. The "being honest about what a referral does and does not mean" section is exactly the right instinct for a medical page. Risk information is present on all three pages, and the outcomes paragraphs lead with the caveat rather than burying it. The BMI framing is correct everywhere.

### What's blocking

**1. The pages disagree with each other, and with themselves, about where surgery happens.** The locations page names St John of God Murdoch and Subiaco in its stats bar, Mount Private and Waikiki in its body copy, and St John of God again in its surgeon profile — three different answers in one document. The other two pages repeat the Mount/Waikiki pair. Neither of those hospitals appears in any brief, on any page already built, or on the live OSWA site. They have been given specific street addresses and links, which makes them read as verified fact.

**2. The second consulting clinic is wrong on all three pages.** They say Subiaco/West Leederville, at an address that appears in no OSWA source. The confirmed second clinic is **Mandurah** (WA Cardiology Centre, 30 Minilya Parkway, Greenfields 6210), settled on 20 July and matching the live site. The likely cause is simple — "Subiaco" is the name of one of the *hospitals*, and it seems to have migrated into the clinic list.

**3. The GP referral page publishes a fax number and an email address.** The brief for that page says twice not to invent them and to direct GPs to phone (08) 9332 0066 instead. This is the page GPs would use to send patient information.

**4. The Medicare position is wrong on the referral page.** It tells patients a referral is what allows them to claim rebates "on specialist consultations and on the procedure itself", and that without one they get nothing. Neither is right: the referral affects the rebate on specialist *consultations*, and a patient without one can still claim at a lower rate. As written it could put someone off proceeding for a financial reason that isn't true. Corrected wording is in the review.

**5. Several sections were never written.** The surgeons page still carries its own brief instructions as body text — "Describe surgery as a powerful tool…", "This section must include a clear compliance caveat. Write about…", "Risk bullet points (4 items):" — and one FAQ answer stops at "He is also...". The cost sections on the other two pages both end mid-sentence. All three pages ship with keyword tables, word-count notes and unchosen "Alt" heading options still in place.

**6. The experience claims need substantiating.** "One of the most experienced bariatric surgeons in Australia", "one of the first surgeons in the country to perform sleeve gastrectomy", "trained many Australian surgeons in the technique" — these appear across all three pages. The underlying facts may well be provable, but they need a citable basis, and the wording should be OSWA's own.

Get us 1 and 2 and the next round on these three should be close to sign-off. The mechanical cleanup — scaffolding, credential spellings, the unfinished sections — is all paste-ready in the individual reviews and is the writer's to action.

### One keyword note

"Best bariatric surgeon perth" runs at **150 searches a month**, and is the highest-demand term across this batch — the surgeons page's own keyword table listed the near-identical phrase at zero. It's also the most competitive cluster OSWA targets anywhere, so that page deserves the most care. Conversely, the locations page targets a hospital term with no measurable volume while ignoring "weight loss clinic perth" (150/month, and the easiest meaningful term available to this site), and the GP referral page's target has no Australian volume at all — it's a genuine patient-journey asset, but it should be scoped as a support page rather than stretched to hit a word count.
