## Design Context

### Product
**GrowthOS** — a diagnostic + operating layer for clinics and service businesses. The thesis:
growth leaks *after* the enquiry. Demand arrives (referrals, ads, SEO, calls, messages) but
leaks when enquiries are missed, delayed, poorly followed up, or untracked. GrowthOS fixes the
layer between demand and revenue: enquiry capture, response speed, booking flow, follow-up,
retention, and visibility. Primary action: **Run the Diagnostic**.

### Users
Owners and managers of clinics and service businesses where every enquiry has real lifetime
value — allied health, dental/cosmetic/wellness, physio/psychology/massage, private clinics,
virtual/mobile health, and coaching/professional services. Time-poor, sceptical of "buy more
leads" advice. The job to be done: see, concretely, where their current enquiries slip away
before spending another dollar on marketing.

### Brand Personality
Direct, diagnostic, operator energy. Numbers and mechanism over promises. Anti-patterns:
agency soft-sell, "we help you grow" platitudes, lead-gen hype. The site demonstrates that
GrowthOS understands the post-enquiry machine better than agencies (who build demand) or CRM
consultants (who configure tools).

### Aesthetic Direction
Premium light editorial. Think Linear / Ramp / Stripe — warm white background (oklch 97%),
charcoal text (oklch 15%), soft grey borders. Restrained, confident, minimal visual noise.
The emerald CTA (oklch(38% 0.18 162)) is the sole accent. **Icons, not imagery** — modern
lucide icons everywhere; the only photograph is Marcus's portrait in the founder section.
Barlow for headings (bold, tight tracking), Figtree for body. Motion is bold but purposeful:
scroll reveals, an enquiry-leak hero motif, animated stat counters, tactile card hovers — all
zero-dependency and reduced-motion safe.

### Design Principles
1. Numbers over claims. If you can say it with a number, don't use prose (e.g. the leak math).
2. One accent. The emerald is sacred. Every other colour serves contrast or recedes.
3. No selling. Demonstrate the mechanism (the leak), don't pitch. Tone stays declarative.
4. Icons earn clarity. Each concept gets one precise icon — never decorative stock imagery.
5. Whitespace is premium. More breathing room = higher perceived value.
6. Motion has a job. Every animation either reveals structure or makes a number land.
