## Design Context

### Users
High-value service business founders and CEOs ($2M–$50M revenue) evaluating a fractional CMO. They are time-poor, outcome-obsessed, and have been burned by agencies before. They browse on desktop during office hours. The job to be done: quickly assess if Marcus is the calibre of person who can actually move their numbers.

### Brand Personality
Direct, data-forward, no-nonsense. Operator energy. Numbers, outcomes, methodology. No fluff. Anti-patterns: agency soft-sell, vague promise language, "we help you grow" platitudes.

### Aesthetic Direction
Premium light editorial. Think Linear / Ramp / Stripe — warm white background (oklch 97%), charcoal text (oklch 15%), soft grey borders. Not a startup SaaS site — sharper, more confident, more restrained. Minimal visual noise. The emerald CTA (oklch(38% 0.18 162)) is the sole accent, used sparingly. Barlow for headings (bold, tight tracking), Figtree for body (clean, readable).

The GrowthOS diagnostic form uses a light paper background — correct for form completion context. Bridge to main site through shared fonts and emerald CTA colour.

### Design Principles
1. Numbers over claims. If you can say it with a number, don't use prose.
2. One accent. The emerald is sacred. Every other colour serves contrast or recedes.
3. No selling. The site demonstrates competence, it doesn't pitch. Tone stays declarative.
4. Portrait earns trust faster than copy. The headshot is a conversion asset, not decoration.
5. Whitespace is premium. More breathing room = higher perceived value.
