## Page purpose
This page settles the practical question every prospective patient has before booking: where will my surgery actually happen, and where will I be seen before and after? It distinguishes the two surgical hospitals (St John of God Murdoch and Subiaco) from the two consulting locations (Booragoon and Mandurah), and gives accreditation, accessibility and referral context. It builds trust through clarity and accredited-hospital signals, not through promises about outcomes. The reader is deciding whether the locations work for their life before they pick up the phone.

### How it works (6 steps)
Reuse the established journey, lightly adapted to surface the locations at the consult and follow-up steps. Two to three sentences each.

1. **GP Referral** — Ask the GP for a referral; enables Medicare rebates and lets the practice review history before the first meeting
2. **Orientation Consultation** — Meet the surgeon at the Booragoon or Mandurah rooms; options explained in plain language, no pressure to decide on the day

### Being honest about what surgery involves (risks)
**Tone:** Calm, direct, frank — same spirit as the surgeons page. Builds trust by not minimising risk.

**Opening paragraph:** State that choosing an accredited private hospital with theatre and ICU backup is part of having surgery done safely, but that no hospital or surgeon can remove the risks surgery carries. The location gives the safest possible setting; it does not change the honest fact that any operation has risks.

**Risk bullet points (4 items):**
- All surgery carries risks: bleeding, infection, blood clots, anaesthetic reactions
- Loose skin after significant weight loss; varies between individuals, sometimes addressable later
- Long-term success depends on sustained dietary/lifestyle change; weight can return without it
- Lifelong vitamin and nutrient monitoring required after some procedures; the team supports this

### Cost paragraph

## Tone and compliance notes
- Use only the confirmed hard facts: hospitals (SJOG Murdoch and Subiaco), consulting addresses (Booragoon — Garden City Specialist Centre, 537 Marmion Street; Mandurah — WA Cardiology Centre, 30 Minilya Pkwy), phone (08) 9332 0066. Do not invent additional addresses, suite numbers, opening hours or specifics.
- Recovery must be phrased as "usually a few nights" — never "3–4 nights" (that specific is unconfirmed).
- No outcome guarantees, no "you will lose X kg", no "guaranteed results".
- No patient testimonials or case studies — AHPRA prohibits testimonial-style claims for regulated health services.
- No "the best", "Perth's leading", "world-class", or superiority claims about the hospitals or the practice — accreditation may be stated factually because it is verifiable.
- All surgery risk information must be present and must not be minimised or buried, even though this is a location page.
- Medicare item numbers may be mentioned factually (publicly available MBS information); cover and eligibility must be described as assessed/clarified, not assumed.
- Voice: direct, credible, clinical — this is a surgical practice, not a wellness brand.
